# CS 614 Applications of Machine Learning  
## Final Project Guidelines

Your final project will be an **application/product pitch**. You may work in groups of **3-4 students**.

Imagine you are pitching a new application to venture capitalists (VCs). Your pitch should:

- **Identify a gap in the market**
- **Identify existing technologies** you plan to leverage
- **Specify modifications** needed to those technologies
- **Provide a "proof of concept"** for your potential investors

> Be creative and have fun!

---

## Technical Details / Guidelines / Recommendations

When developing your idea and application, consider one of the following technical approaches:

- **Train a model from scratch** (in PyTorch) using an appropriate architecture (from prior work) with a custom dataset (or an existing one, if appropriate)
- **Fine-tune an existing model** using a dataset different from what it was originally trained on
- **Use an existing model** for a unique or novel application

---

## Deliverables

- **Presentation slidedeck**
- **Proof of Concept Demo**

Below are more details about each component:

---

### Presentation Slidedeck

During finals week, you will create a video presentation, culminating in a proof of concept demo.

Your presentation should include:

- **Problem Statement:**  
  - What are you proposing and why?  
  - Where are the gaps?  
  - What exists currently?
- **Technical Information:**  
  - **Model:** Is it existing? What is the architecture?  
  - **Training:** Dataset information & statistics
- **Demo!:**  
  - Screenshots and/or mockups of use cases

---

### Demo

We do **not** expect a full application (front end + back end, etc.).  
We want to see a **"proof of concept" demo**. What your demo includes can vary widely based on your project.
